2. Xīngqīrì shì jǐ hào? (What date is Sunday?)
3. Tā jīnnián duō dà? (How old is he this year?)

6. Wǒ mǎi yí ge dà dàngāo, hǎo ma? (I'll buy a big cake, okay? [Polite Proposal])
7. Zhù nǐ shēngri kuàilè! (Wish you a happy birthday! ["zhù" + person + wish])
8. Wǒ hěn xǐhuan chī kǎoyā. (I really like eating roast duck.)
🎯 Question Words (疑问代词) Quick Reference
nǎ nián / jǐ yuè jǐ hào / nǎ tiān
Used for Year, Date, and Specific Day. (e.g., Tā nǎ nián chūshēng? / Jīntiān shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào?)
xīngqī jǐ / shénme shíhou / jǐ diǎn
Used for Weekday, Time in general, and Clock Time. (e.g., Míngtiān shì xīngqī jǐ? / Nǐ jǐ diǎn yǒu kè?)
duō dà vs jǐ suì
"Duō dà" for adults/general age; "Jǐ suì" specifically for children under 10.
nǎr vs shǔ shénme
"Nǎr rén" asks for hometown/city; "Shǔ shénme" asks for Chinese Zodiac animal.

Grammar Rules & Deep Dive

1. Expressing Dates: BIG to SMALL Order Pg 141

Always arrange time units from largest to smallest: Year -> Month -> Day -> Day of Week -> Time of Day -> Hour/Minute.

✔ 2026 nián, shíyuè, èrshíqī hào, xīngqīsì, shàngwǔ 9 diǎn
✘ 9 AM, Thursday, October 27th, 2026 (Never use English word order!)
2. Time Expressions: "shàng" vs "xià" Comparative Matrix Pg 142
Time Unit Previous (shàng / 过去) Current (zhè / jīn / 现在) Next (xià / míng / 未来)
Month (yuè) shàng ge yuè (last month) zhè ge yuè (this month) xià ge yuè (next month)
Week (xīngqī) shàng xīngqī (last week) zhè xīngqī (this week) xià xīngqī (next week)
Year (nián) ⚠️ No shàng/xià! qùnián (last year) jīnnián (this year) míngnián (next year)
Day (tiān) ⚠️ No shàng/xià! zuótiān (yesterday) jīntiān / xiànzài (today / now) míngtiān (tomorrow)
⚠️ CRUCIAL PITFALL: Never say "shàng nián" or "xià tiān"!
3. Age Expression: Nominal Predicate (名词谓语句) Pg 143

When stating age, the noun phrase functions directly as the predicate. DO NOT insert "shì"!

✔ Tā jīnnián èrshí suì.
✘ Tā jīnnián shì èrshí suì. (Incorrect!)

5. Extended Usage: "#19 guò" & "... de shíhou"
1. "guò" (to celebrate): guò shēngri = celebrate a birthday.

2. "... de shíhou" (when... / during...):
• Zhōngguó rén guò shēngri de shíhou chī dàngāo ma? (Do Chinese people eat cake WHEN celebrating birthdays?)
• Mèimei chànggē de shíhou hěn kě'ài. (WHEN younger sister sings, she is very cute.)
• Chī hànbǎo de shíhou wǒ yào hē kělè. (WHEN eating a hamburger, I want to drink cola.)
6. Distinguishing "zhùhè" vs "zhù"
• zhùhè (congratulate on an event): Verb + Event ➔ e.g., Wǒmen zhùhè tā de shēngri. (We congratulate his birthday.)
• zhù (wish someone something): Verb + Person + Wish ➔ e.g., Zhù nǐ shēngri kuàilè! (Wish you a happy birthday!)

Core Dialogue Breakdown & Key Notes

Text 1 Breakdown: Appointment & Birthday Plan Pg 130-131
Key Scene 1 (Lines 1-6): Checking Schedule
• Míngtiān shàngwǔ nǐ yǒu méiyǒu kè? (Affirmative-Negative Question for schedule)
• Wǒ shàngwǔ, xiàwǔ dōu yǒu kè. (Use "dōu" to emphasize "both morning and afternoon")
Key Scene 2 (Lines 7-13): Birthday Details & Hometown
• Xīngqīrì shì shíyuè èrshíqī hào, shì Sòng Huá de shēngri.
• Sòng Huá 1982 nián shíyuè èrshíqī rì chūshēng, shǔ gǒu. Tā jīnnián èrshí suì.
• Tā shì Běijīng rén. (Asking hometown: "Tā shì nǎr rén?")
Key Scene 3 (Lines 14-17): Party Preparations & Proposal
• Zhōngguó rén guò shēngri de shíhou chī dàngāo ma? (Time clause: "...de shíhou")
• Wǒ mǎi yí ge dà dàngāo, hǎo ma? (Making a proposal: "... , hǎo ma?")
• Wǒ mǎi liǎng píng hóng pútaojiǔ. (Quantifier structure: Number + MW + Noun)
Text 2 Breakdown: Celebration Party Pg 134-135
Key Expressions during the Party:
1. Wishes: Zhù nǐ shēngri kuàilè! (Happy Birthday!)
2. Compliments: Dàngāo zhēn piàoliang. (The cake is really beautiful.)
3. Food Specialties: Jīntiān wǒmen chī Běijīng kǎoyā... wǒmen hái chī shòumiàn. (Longevity noodles symbolizing long life)
4. Future Action: Shíyīyuè shí'èr hào wǒmen zài lái chī shòumiàn. (Use "zài" + Verb for future repetition)

3. Answer the Questions Pg 139
1. Q: Yì nián yǒu jǐ ge yuè? (How many months in a year?)
Ans: Yì nián yǒu shí'èr ge yuè. (There are 12 months in a year.)
2. Q: Yì nián yǒu duōshao ge xīngqī? (How many weeks in a year?)
Ans: Yì nián yǒu wǔshí'èr ge xīngqī. (There are 52 weeks in a year.)
3. Q: Yí ge xīngqī yǒu jǐ tiān? (How many days in a week?)
Ans: Yí ge xīngqī yǒu qī tiān. (There are 7 days in a week.)
4. Q: Shíyīyuè yǒu duōshao tiān? (How many days in November?)
Ans: Shíyīyuè yǒu sānshí tiān. (There are 30 days in November.)
5. Q: Jīntiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào? Xīngqī jǐ? (What date and weekday is today?)
Ans: Jīntiān [Month] yuè [Day] hào, xīngqī [Weekday]. (Today is [Month] [Day], [Weekday].)
6. Q: Míngtiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào? Xīngqī jǐ? (What date and weekday is tomorrow?)
Ans: Míngtiān [Month] yuè [Day] hào, xīngqī [Weekday]. (Tomorrow is [Month] [Day], [Weekday].)
7. Q: Xīngqī yǒu jǐ tiān? (How many days in a week?)
Ans: Xīngqī yǒu qī tiān. (There are 7 days in a week.)
8. Q: Xīngqīrì nǐ zuò shénme? (What do you do on Sunday?)
Ans: Xīngqīrì wǒ cānjiā jùhuì / qù péngyou jiā. (I attend a party / go to a friend's house on Sunday.)
9. Q: Nǐ xǐhuan chī shénme? Xǐhuan chī Zhōngcān ma? Xǐhuan chī Xīcān ma? (What do you like to eat? Do you like Chinese food? Do you like Western food?)
Ans: Wǒ xǐhuan chī kǎoyā. Wǒ xǐhuan chī Zhōngcān, yě xǐhuan chī Xīcān. (I like eating roast duck. I like Chinese food, and also like Western food.)
10. Q: Nǐ xǐhuan hē chá ma? (Do you like drinking tea?)
Ans: Wǒ xǐhuan hē chá. (I like drinking tea.)
11. Q: Nǐ xǐhuan chī mǐfàn ma? (Do you like eating rice?)
Ans: Wǒ xǐhuan chī mǐfàn. (I like eating rice.)
