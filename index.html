1. Míngtiān shàngwǔ nǐ yǒu méiyǒu kè?
(Do you have class tomorrow morning?)
2. Xīngqīrì shì jǐ hào?
(What date is Sunday?)
3. Tā jīnnián duō dà?
(How old is he this year?)
4. Tā yī jiǔ bā èr nián shíyuè èrshíqī rì chūshēng.
(He was born on October 27, 1982.)
5. Tā jīnnián èrshí suì.
(He is 20 years old this year. [Nominal Predicate - NO "shì"!])
6. Wǒ mǎi yí ge dà dàngāo, hǎo ma?
(I'll buy a big cake, okay? [Polite Proposal])
7. Zhù nǐ shēngri kuàilè!
(Wish you a happy birthday! ["zhù" + person + wish])
8. Wǒ hěn xǐhuan chī kǎoyā.
(I love to eat roast duck.)
❓ Question Words Quick Reference
• nǎ nián / jǐ yuè jǐ hào / nǎ tiān ➔ Used for Year, Date, and Specific Day.
(e.g., Tā nǎ nián chūshēng? / Jīntiān shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào?)
• xīngqī jǐ / shénme shíhou / jǐ diǎn ➔ Used for Weekday, General Time, and Exact Clock Time.
(e.g., Míngtiān shì xīngqī jǐ? / Nǐ jǐ diǎn yǒu kè?)
• duō dà vs jǐ suì ➔ "duō dà" for adults/general age; "jǐ suì" specifically for children under 10.
• nǎr rén vs shǔ shénme ➔ "nǎr rén" asks for hometown/city; "shǔ shénme" asks for Chinese Zodiac animal.

Grammar Key Points & Rules

1. Expressing Dates: BIG to SMALL Order Pg 141

Always arrange time units from largest to smallest: Year ➔ Month ➔ Day ➔ Day of Week ➔ Time of Day ➔ Hour/Minute.

✔ 2026 nián, shíyuè, èrshíqī hào, xīngqīsì, shàngwǔ 9 diǎn
✘ 9 AM, Thursday, October 27th, 2026 (Never use English word order!)
2. Usage Matrix for "shàng" & "xià" in Time Expressions Pg 142

Concept arranged by Time Sequence: Past/Previous ➔ Present ➔ Future/Next.

Time Unit Previous / Before (shàng) Present / Now Next / After (xià)
Year (nián) ✘ shàng nián
➔ qùnián (last year) 		jīnnián (this year) ✘ xià nián
➔ míngnián (next year)
Month (yuè) shàng ge yuè (last month) zhè ge yuè (this month) xià ge yuè (next month)
Week (xīngqī) shàng xīngqī (last week) zhè xīngqī (this week) xià xīngqī (next week)
Day (tiān) ✘ shàng tiān
➔ zuótiān (yesterday) 		jīntiān (today) ✘ xià tiān
➔ míngtiān (tomorrow)
Time of Day shàngwǔ (morning) xiànzài (now) xiàwǔ (afternoon)
3. Age Expression: Nominal Predicate Pg 143

When stating age, the numeral-measure phrase functions directly as the predicate. DO NOT insert "shì"!

✔ Tā jīnnián èrshí suì.
✘ Tā jīnnián shì èrshí suì. (Incorrect!)

5. Extended Usage: "guò" & "... de shíhou"
1. "guò" (to celebrate): guò shēngri = celebrate a birthday.

2. "... de shíhou" (when... / during...):
• Zhōngguó rén guò shēngri de shíhou chī dàngāo ma? (Do Chinese people eat cake WHEN celebrating birthdays?)
• Mèimei chànggē de shíhou hěn kě'ài. (WHEN younger sister sings, she is very cute.)
• Chī hànbǎo de shíhou wǒ yào hē kělè. (WHEN eating a hamburger, I want to drink cola.)
6. Distinguishing "zhùhè" vs "zhù"
• zhùhè (congratulate on an event): Verb + Event
e.g., Wǒmen zhùhè tā de shēngri. (We congratulate his birthday.)
• zhù (wish someone something): Verb + Person + Wish
e.g., Zhù nǐ shēngri kuàilè! (Wish you a happy birthday!)

Core Dialogue Breakdown & Key Notes

Text 1 Breakdown: Appointment & Birthday Plan Pg 130-131
Key Scene 1 (Lines 1-6): Checking Schedule
• Míngtiān shàngwǔ nǐ yǒu méiyǒu kè? (Affirmative-Negative Question for schedule)
• Wǒ shàngwǔ, xiàwǔ dōu yǒu kè. (Use "dōu" to emphasize "both morning and afternoon")
Key Scene 2 (Lines 7-13): Birthday Details & Hometown
• Xīngqīrì shì shíyuè èrshíqī hào, shì Sòng Huá de shēngri.
• Sòng Huá 1982 nián shíyuè èrshíqī rì chūshēng, shǔ gǒu. Tā jīnnián èrshí suì.
• Tā shì Běijīng rén. (Asking hometown: "Tā shì nǎr rén?")
Key Scene 3 (Lines 14-17): Party Preparations & Proposal
• Zhōngguó rén guò shēngri de shíhou chī dàngāo ma? (Time clause: "...de shíhou")
• Wǒ mǎi yí ge dà dàngāo, hǎo ma? (Making a proposal: "... , hǎo ma?")
• Wǒ mǎi liǎng píng hóng pútaojiǔ. (Quantifier structure: Number + MW + Noun)
Text 2 Breakdown: Celebration Party Pg 134-135
Key Expressions during the Party:
1. Wishes: Zhù nǐ shēngri kuàilè! (Happy Birthday!)
2. Compliments: Dàngāo zhēn piàoliang. (The cake is really beautiful.)
3. Food Specialties: Jīntiān wǒmen chī Běijīng kǎoyā... wǒmen hái chī shòumiàn. (Longevity noodles symbolizing long life)
4. Future Action: Shíyīyuè shí'èr hào wǒmen zài lái chī shòumiàn. (Use "zài" + Verb for future repetition)
